# **Global-Local Task**

![Global-Local](https://github.com/ThipkanlayaJaiaue/adaptive-executive-function-tasks/blob/main/Switching-GlobalLocal/globalLocal.gif)

### Task Configuration

The task included two trial types:

- **Switch trials:** The Navon figure changed colour from the previous trial, requiring participants to switch between the global task (classifying the global shape) and the local task (classifying the local shape), or vice versa.
- **Repeat trials:** The Navon figure retained the same colour, requiring participants to repeat the same task.

The task included three versions:

- **Control:** One switch trial presented midway through the task, between two randomly ordered sets of repeat trials (one for the global task and one for the local task). Fixed trial duration based on familiarisation.
- **Standard:** Equal numbers of switch and repeat trials (excluding the first trial). Fixed trial duration based on familiarisation.
- **Adaptive:** Equal numbers of switch and repeat trials (excluding the first trial). Trial duration was initially set based on familiarisation and adjusted for the subsequent trial based on performance on switch trials.

**Trial randomisation:** Trials were presented in random order, subject to the following constraints:

- The same Navon figure could not be presented on consecutive trials.
- No more than two consecutive global or local trials could share the same response key.
- Each shape (triangle, cross, square, and circle) appeared in at least 20% of trials overall, and at least 10% of trials independently within the global and local tasks.
- **Standard and adaptive versions:** No more than two consecutive switch or repeat trials (i.e., a maximum of three consecutive global or local trials).
